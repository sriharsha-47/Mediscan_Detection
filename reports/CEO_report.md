**What it must not be used for.** Diagnosis; adults; PA views; other X-ray machines/hospitals without re-validation (external hospital: AUC fell from __ to __, results/external_summary.md); anything where a clinician is not in the loop; as a reason to skip a child with symptoms.
**Why "99% accuracy" is the wrong target.** At 9:1 prevalence a tool that never flags anyone is 90% accurate. Per 1,000 children a 99%-accurate tool may still miss 10 sick children (cost 200) — or raise 10 false alarms (cost 10); accuracy cannot tell them apart (table: results/ceo_arithmetic.md). **Ask instead for:** sensitivity ≥ 95% (lower CI bound reported) at a stated specificity, evaluated on external-hospital data, with a "refer to clinician" band.
